Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026.

Skylight Atlas ("the app") is a private, single-user application. It is used only by its owner and does not collect, process, or share data from any other person.

What data is accessed

The app reads only the owner's own social media data — for TikTok, the owner's own profile information, follower and engagement counts, and the list of the owner's own videos with their public metrics — obtained through TikTok's official API after the owner authorizes access to their own account.

How it is stored and used

The data is stored privately in the owner's own self-hosted database and shown only to the owner in their own analytics dashboard. It is never sold, shared, or made available to any third party or other user.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls all stored data and can delete it at any time. Access can be revoked at any time from the connected platform's settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the operator at the email associated with this GitHub account.
